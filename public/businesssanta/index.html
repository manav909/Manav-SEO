      Questions people ask

      Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

      No honest SEO can guarantee a position, because Google decides rankings. We guarantee the work: a clear plan, every fix done and shown to you, and reports you can check in Google Search Console.

      How do we get started?

      Message us on WhatsApp with your website and goal. We review the site, tell you what it needs and what it costs, and start once you agree.

      Do you use AI to do the work?

      We use AI tools to speed up research, the same way we use other SEO software. People decide what to do, write or review every change, and check it before it goes live. We never put your logins, customer data or private analytics into public AI chat tools.

      Is my website and data safe with you?

      You keep full ownership of your site, content and accounts. We ask only for the access a task needs, you can remove it when we finish, and we do not share your information with anyone.

      Who will I talk to?

      Jyoti is your main contact for questions, quotes and calls. Sam handles technical SEO and Search Console, Sakshi handles on-page work, content, WordPress and Shopify, and Manav leads strategy, AI search and bigger projects. The right person joins your conversation when your question needs them.

      Which platforms do you work with?

      WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and custom-built sites.

      What is GEO, and do I need it?

      Generative Engine Optimization makes your business easier for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude and Gemini to find, understand and cite. More buyers now ask an AI before they search, so it helps to be the answer it gives.

      How soon will I see results?

      Technical fixes usually show in Search Console within a few weeks. Rankings and traffic build over months and depend on your competition. We tell you what to expect for your site before you pay.
