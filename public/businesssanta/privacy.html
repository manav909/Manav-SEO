Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Business Santa collects through this page and how we use it.

What we collect

The site has no account system and stores no form data on our servers. When you use the form or a WhatsApp button, your name, website and the service you chose are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. When you message us, we receive your WhatsApp number, name and what you wrote.

If we have turned on the Meta Pixel for our advertising, Meta may record that you visited this page or opened a WhatsApp chat, using cookies or similar technology, so we can measure our ads. Meta's use of that data is covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer you, review your website, prepare a quote and deliver the work you order. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

WhatsApp (Meta) carries our conversations. We share nothing else with third parties unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations and project notes for as long as we work together, and for a reasonable time after, so we can support you. Ask us at any time to delete your information and we will.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know and to delete; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the Meta measurement described above, and you can opt out of that by blocking cookies or using your browser's privacy settings.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Message us on WhatsApp from the home page or on Instagram at @businesssanta.
