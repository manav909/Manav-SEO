## Every episode carries

- Cold open on the surprise in the first 5 seconds.
- End hook with one ask: "Comment CHECK and your city" or the episode's one-word question, plus subscribe.
- A pinned comment from the channel asking the same question.
- Description: hook line, comment ask, chapters, free tools, Talk to us (email, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook), about Business Santa powered by SEO Season, method with dates, hashtags.
- End screen (subscribe plus a video or playlist) on the last 12 to 15 seconds; the end card leaves space for it.
- Cross-posting: the Short on YouTube and as the Reel on Facebook and Instagram, a LinkedIn showcase post, and the episode on businesssanta.com/videos plus the matching industry page.
