Get your business recommended by ChatGPT and AI search

AI search optimization, also called GEO (generative engine optimization), is the work of making sure AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity know your business and name it when people ask for a recommendation. They build answers from what is published about you across the web, so the work is about being described clearly, consistently and often in the places they read.

How AI assistants choose a business

They read the web, not your mind

AI assistants answer from sources they can find: your website, your listings on Google, Bing and Apple Maps, review sites, industry directories and local articles.

They trust what agrees

If your name, address, services and hours are the same everywhere, assistants are more confident about you. Conflicting details make them skip you.

They quote clear answers

Pages that answer common questions directly, in plain words, are easier for AI to use than vague marketing copy.

They like proof

Detailed reviews, mentions in local "best of" lists and articles give assistants a reason to recommend you over others.

Common questions

What is GEO?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. It means improving how AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews find, understand and recommend your business.

Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap a lot. Good SEO makes your business easy to find and understand. GEO adds a focus on clear answers, consistent facts across the web and mentions in the sources AI assistants rely on.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No one can. AI answers change and depend on the question. We make your business as clear and well supported as possible, and we show you what the assistants say before and after.
