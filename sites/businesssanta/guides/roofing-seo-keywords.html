Roofing SEO keywords: what homeowners really search

The best roofing SEO keywords are the ones homeowners type when they are ready to hire: "roofer near me", "roof leak repair", "roof replacement cost", "storm damage roof repair" and the same searches with your town's name. Group them by the job they describe, give each group its own page, and use the homeowner's words in your titles, headings and Google Business Profile services.

How to use them

  1. One page per job groupA page for repairs, one for replacements, one for storm damage, one for inspections. Each answers the questions behind its searches.
  2. One page per town you really serveReal jobs, photos and reviews from that town. Thin pages that only swap the town name do not help.
  3. Match your Google Business Profile servicesList the same services on your profile, in the same words homeowners use.
  4. Use the words in titles and headings"Roof leak repair in Plano" beats "Quality roofing solutions".
  5. Answer the cost questionsHomeowners search for prices. Give honest ranges or what changes the price, even if you quote each job.

Common questions

What is the most important roofing keyword?

Usually "roofer near me" and "roof repair near me". They show up on Google Maps, so they depend mostly on your Google Business Profile, reviews and how close you are.

Should I target every town around me?

Only the towns you really serve and can prove with jobs and reviews. Start with the ones that matter most to your business.

Do I need a separate page for each keyword?

No. One page per job group or town is enough. Similar searches like "roof leak repair" and "fix leaking roof" belong on the same page.
