Storm season SEO: how roofers get the calls first

Roofers who get the most leads after a storm prepare before it: storm damage and insurance pages already live, a Google Business Profile with storm services listed and fresh photos, and a fast way to request an inspection. In the storm week, post your availability, answer every call and message quickly, and afterwards ask every customer for a review that mentions the storm work.

The list

  1. Before the season: build the pagesStorm damage repair, hail damage, wind damage, emergency tarping and insurance claim help, each on its own page with real photos.
  2. Before the season: update your profileAdd the storm services to your Google Business Profile, refresh photos, and check your hours and phone.
  3. Before the season: make booking easyA short inspection form and a tap-to-call button at the top of every page.
  4. Storm week: post your availabilityA Google Business Profile post and a website banner saying you are taking inspections in the affected towns.
  5. Storm week: answer fastHomeowners call several roofers. The first one to answer usually gets the inspection.
  6. Storm week: be clear and honestExplain your inspection, your licenses and how insurance claims work. Follow your state's rules on insurance claims and deductibles.
  7. After: ask for reviewsA review that says "repaired our hail damage in two days" helps you show up the next time a storm hits.
  8. After: add the jobs to your town pagesPhotos and short write-ups of storm repairs make your town pages stronger for the next season.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: How to improve your local ranking on Google.

Common questions

When should a roofer prepare for storm season?

A few months before your local hail or hurricane season. Pages and profile changes need time to be found and ranked.

Do storm damage pages really help?

Yes. Searches like "hail damage roof repair" spike right after storms, and the roofers with a clear page for it are the ones that show up.

Should I run ads during a storm?

Ads can help in a short spike. SEO makes sure you also show up in the free map and search results, which many homeowners trust more.
