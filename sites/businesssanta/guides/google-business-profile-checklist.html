Google Business Profile checklist: 15 steps to show up on Maps

To optimize your Google Business Profile, verify it, choose the most accurate primary category, fill in every service and attribute, keep hours current (including holidays), add real photos regularly, get a steady flow of reviews and reply to them, and keep your name, address and phone exactly the same everywhere online. Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and a complete, active profile improves the two you control.

The list

  1. Claim and verify your profileAn unverified profile cannot rank well or be edited safely. Verify at business.google.com.
  2. Use your real business nameExactly as it appears on your sign and paperwork. Adding keywords to the name breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.
  3. Pick the most accurate primary categoryThis is one of the strongest relevance signals. Choose what you are, not what you wish to rank for.
  4. Add secondary categoriesOnly for things you really do, like Water heater installation for a plumber.
  5. List every serviceAdd each service with a short plain description. Services help Google match you to specific searches.
  6. Set your service area or address correctlyIf customers come to you, show your address. If you go to them, set a service area and hide the address.
  7. Keep hours accurate, including holidaysWrong hours cost visits and reviews. Add special hours for every holiday.
  8. Write a clear descriptionSay what you do, where, and for whom, in plain words. No links or promotions.
  9. Add photos every monthReal photos of your work, team, inside and outside. Fresh photos show the business is active.
  10. Turn on messaging and bookingLet people message you or book directly if you can respond quickly.
  11. Ask every happy customer for a reviewSend a short link by text right after the job. A steady flow of recent reviews matters more than one burst.
  12. Reply to every reviewThank people by name, keep replies short, and handle complaints calmly. Never share private details.
  13. Answer common questionsAdd the questions customers ask most to your website's FAQ and make sure your profile details answer them too.
  14. Post updatesOffers, events and seasonal news. Posts keep your profile active and give people a reason to call.
  15. Match your details everywhereSame name, address and phone on your website, Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and directories. Mismatches weaken trust.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: How to improve your local ranking on Google.

Common questions

How long does it take for profile changes to help?

Some changes, like categories and hours, can affect results within days or weeks. Reviews and photos build their effect over months.

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free.

What is the most important part of the profile?

Your primary category, followed by complete services, accurate hours and a steady flow of reviews.
