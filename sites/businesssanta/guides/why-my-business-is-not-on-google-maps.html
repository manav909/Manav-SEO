Why isn't my business showing up on Google Maps?

A business usually does not show up on Google Maps because its Google Business Profile is not verified, is suspended, uses the wrong primary category, is too far from the person searching, has few reviews, or has details that do not match across the web. Search your exact business name first: if you appear, the problem is ranking, not visibility, and the fixes below apply.

The list

  1. The profile is not verifiedUnverified profiles often do not show at all. Check business.google.com and finish verification.
  2. The profile was suspendedUsually from keywords in the business name, a virtual office address or a rule break. Fix the cause and request reinstatement.
  3. You are searching from too far awayGoogle favors businesses close to the searcher. Check results from inside your service area, not from home.
  4. The wrong primary categoryIf your category does not match the search, you will not show for it. Choose the most accurate one.
  5. Missing services and detailsEmpty services, hours and descriptions give Google little to match against searches.
  6. Too few reviewsCompetitors with more recent reviews look more prominent. Ask every happy customer, every time.
  7. Details that do not matchDifferent names, addresses or phone numbers on different sites confuse Google. Make them identical.
  8. A duplicate listingTwo profiles for one business split your signals. Report or merge the duplicate.
  9. A new or inactive profileNew profiles take time. Add photos and posts regularly to show the business is active.
  10. A weak websiteYour website supports your profile. A slow site with one vague page does not help Google understand what you do.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: How to improve your local ranking on Google.

Common questions

Why does my business show up on my phone but not my customer's?

Results depend on where the person searching is. You may appear near your location but not near theirs. Check from inside your service area.

How do I check if my profile is suspended?

Sign in at business.google.com. A suspended profile shows a notice at the top of the dashboard.

Can I pay Google to show up on Maps?

You can run ads that appear on Maps, but the regular map results cannot be bought. They are earned with a complete profile, reviews and a good website.
