Local SEO services for small businesses

Local SEO is the work that gets your business into the results people see when they search for something near them, like "plumber near me" or "best tacos in Austin". It covers your Google Business Profile, your position on Google Maps, your website, your reviews, your listings across the web, and now the answers AI assistants like ChatGPT give.

Most people choose from the first few businesses they see. Local SEO is how you become one of them, without paying for every click.

What local SEO includes

Google Business Profile

The box with your hours, photos, reviews and directions. We fill in every field, pick the right categories and keep it fresh.

Google Maps ranking

The three businesses on the map get most of the calls. Google ranks them on relevance, distance and prominence, and we work on the two you can change.

Website fixes

Fast on a phone, a page for each service, clear contact details, and technical fixes so Google can read every page.

Reviews

A simple way to get more reviews and reply to them well. Reviews are one of the strongest local signals.

Listings and citations

Your name, address and phone the same on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and the directories for your industry.

AI search (GEO)

Making sure ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity can find and describe your business correctly.

How Google picks the top 3 on the map

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. Distance is fixed by where you are. Relevance and prominence are what local SEO improves: a complete Google Business Profile, pages for each service, reviews, and the same details everywhere online.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: How to improve your local ranking on Google.
