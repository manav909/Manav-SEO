Law firm SEO for attorneys who want the call

Law firm SEO means getting your firm into the top results when people nearby search "lawyer near me" or a practice area like "car accident lawyer" or "family lawyer near me". For most firms that starts with a strong Google Business Profile, a clear page for each practice area, and reviews, all within your state bar's advertising rules.

A new client usually needs a lawyer this week, and they pick from the few firms they see first. They look for the right practice area, good reviews and a free consultation. If your firm does not show up there, the call goes to someone else.

What we fix for law firms

A strong Google Business Profile

The right primary category for your main practice, each practice area listed, office photos, and a clear consultation booking link.

A page for each practice area

Car accidents, divorce, estate planning, criminal defense. People search for the problem they have, so each practice area needs its own page that answers their first questions.

Content that follows bar rules

Clear, accurate descriptions with no promises of outcomes. We avoid words like "specialist" unless your state allows it, and we keep your required disclaimers in place.

Reviews, handled carefully

A simple way to ask clients for reviews, and replies that never reveal private case details.

A website that converts

Fast on a phone, a tap-to-call button at the top, and a short consultation form. Many legal searches happen on phones, often at night.

Matching listings

Your firm name, address and phone the same on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Avvo, Justia, FindLaw and Super Lawyers, the directories AI assistants read.

How Google picks the top 3 on the map

Google ranks local results on three things: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the person searching) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed you are). You cannot move your shop, so we work on relevance and prominence: a complete profile, pages that match each service, steady reviews and consistent listings.

Questions law firms ask us

Profile and listing fixes can help within weeks. Competitive practice areas in big cities can take six months or more. Smaller towns and narrower practice areas often move faster.

Yes. We write plain, accurate content, avoid outcome promises and keep your disclaimers. Your firm approves everything before it goes live, and you remain responsible for final compliance.

Can solo attorneys benefit?

Yes. Solo and small firms often win locally because they are close to the searcher and have personal reviews. A complete profile and clear practice pages go a long way.

Yes. We write them in plain English so clients understand them, and you review them before anything is published.
