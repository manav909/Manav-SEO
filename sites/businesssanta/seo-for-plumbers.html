SEO for plumbers that makes the phone ring

SEO for plumbers means getting your plumbing business into the top results when someone nearby searches "plumber near me" or "emergency plumber open now". Most of those calls go to the three businesses Google shows on the map, so the work starts with your Google Business Profile, then your website, your reviews and the listings that AI assistants read.

Plumbing calls are urgent. A homeowner with a burst pipe does not compare ten websites. They tap the first business on the map that looks open, trusted and close. Our job is to make that business yours.

What we fix for plumbers

Your Google Business Profile, filled in properly

Primary category set to Plumber, every service you offer listed (water heaters, drains, repiping, sewer lines), your real service area, and hours that match when you actually answer the phone.

24/7 and emergency signals

If you take emergency calls, we make sure Google and your website both say so clearly, so you show up for "open now" and "emergency plumber" searches at night and on weekends.

A page for each service and town

One clear page for water heater repair, one for drain cleaning, and pages for the towns you serve. Google matches searches to pages, so a single "Services" page misses most of them.

A website that gets the call

A tap-to-call button at the top, a fast load on a phone, and your number the same everywhere. A slow site loses the caller to the next plumber on the list.

Reviews that mention the job

A simple text link that asks happy customers for a review. Reviews that say "fixed our water heater the same day" help you show up for those exact searches.

Listings that match

Your name, address and phone the same on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and the directories AI assistants read. Mismatches make search engines less sure about you.

How Google picks the top 3 on the map

Google ranks local results on three things: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the person searching) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed you are). You cannot move your shop, so we work on relevance and prominence: a complete profile, pages that match each service, steady reviews and consistent listings.

Questions plumbers ask us

How long does SEO take for a plumbing business?

Fixes to your Google Business Profile can start helping within a few weeks. Ranking higher for competitive searches like "plumber near me" usually builds over three to six months, depending on your town and your competitors.

Should plumbers use Google Local Services Ads too?

They work well together. Local Services Ads are paid and show above everything else. SEO brings the free calls from the map and the regular results. Many plumbers use both, and a strong profile helps both.

Can you help if I only serve customers at their homes?

Yes. Most plumbers are service-area businesses. We set up your profile with a service area instead of a public address, which is how Google wants it done.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. We fix the one you have so it loads fast on phones, shows your number at the top and has a page for each service you want calls for.
