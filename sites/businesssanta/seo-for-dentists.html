Dental SEO for practices that want new patients

Dental SEO means getting your practice into the top results when people nearby search "dentist near me", "emergency dentist today" or "dentist taking new patients". The biggest wins usually come from a complete Google Business Profile, a page for each treatment, steady patient reviews, and listings that match across the web.

People choose a dentist on trust. They read reviews, look at photos of the office and check whether you take their insurance before they ever call. If that information is missing, they book somewhere else.

What we fix for dentists

A complete Google Business Profile

The right primary category (Dentist, or your specialty), every treatment listed, office photos, accessibility details, and new-patient booking links.

A page for each treatment

Cleanings, implants, crowns, Invisalign, emergency visits. Patients search for the treatment, so each one needs its own clear page.

Insurance and new-patient answers

The plans you accept and whether you take new patients, written plainly on your site. These are some of the first things people ask Google and AI assistants.

Review replies that protect patient privacy

We help you ask for reviews and reply to them without confirming that someone is a patient or mentioning treatment. HHS has fined dental practices for revealing patient information in review replies.

A fast, easy website

Online booking or a tap-to-call button at the top, fast loading on a phone, and your hours clear on every page.

Matching listings

Your practice name, address and phone the same on Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp and other directories that search engines and AI read.

How Google picks the top 3 on the map

Google ranks local results on three things: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the person searching) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed you are). You cannot move your shop, so we work on relevance and prominence: a complete profile, pages that match each service, steady reviews and consistent listings.

Source: Google Business Profile Help: How to improve your local ranking on Google.

Yes. Orthodontists, oral surgeons, pediatric dentists and cosmetic practices all follow the same approach, with pages built around their own treatments.
